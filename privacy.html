Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Clutch Up Golf ("Clutch Up", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, first and last name, username, gender and Handicap Index. If you sign up with email, your password is stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see it. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and either your email or a private relay address ("Hide My Email"); we don't receive your Apple password.
  • Golf data: rounds, scores, games, matchups and teams; events, trips and seasons (including Points Race and individual standings); course corrections and courses you add; optional stats you choose to track (putts, fairways, greens); and stories written about your matches and events.
  • Trip expenses: if a trip organizer turns on expense tracking, the bills people add: what it was for, the amount and currency, who paid and who it's split between.
  • Friends and requests: your friends list, friend requests, requests to join an event, requests to enter scores in a match, handicap update requests, and people you block or report.
  • Scorecard photos: only when you choose to scan or upload a scorecard. The photo is sent to be read and is not kept after the scores or course details are returned.
  • Course searches: the course names or cities you search for, so we can find courses.
  • Location (optional): only if you allow it, and only while the app is open, your approximate location is used to show golf courses near you and to sort course searches by distance. It's rounded to about 1 km before it leaves your phone, used for that one search, and never stored or linked to your account. You can turn it off any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, a report with the error, your phone model and the app version is sent so we can fix it. It doesn't include your name, email or scores.
  • Subscriptions: whether you have Clutch Up Pro and when it renews or ends. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send notifications you've allowed, including live scores on your Lock Screen.
  • Anonymous usage data: which features are used (for example "round created" or which screen was opened), with non-personal details like the game type or number of players, your phone type and the app version. It's tied to a random ID for your copy of the app, not to your account, and never includes your name, email, username, scores or location. You can turn this off in More → Account Settings → Preferences.
  • Guests can follow rounds and events without an email address; their data is tied to an anonymous account.

We don't store your location, and we don't collect your contacts or advertising identifiers or track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run the app: keep score, share rounds and events with the people in them, and show results, standings, records and stories.
  • To work out trip expenses: who owes whom and the simplest way to settle up.
  • To send notifications you've allowed (for example, a friend asking to join your event, or a live score update).
  • To provide Clutch Up Pro features you've subscribed to.
  • To keep the community safe (reports and blocks) and to provide support.
  • To understand which features are used, so we know what to improve (anonymous usage data).

We don't sell your personal information and we don't show ads.

Who can see your information

  • Other players: your name, username and Handicap Index can be found by other users searching for friends. Rounds and events are shared with the people in them and with anyone they give the join code to. Trip expenses are visible to the people on that trip.
  • Event organizers: when you ask to join an event, its organizer sees your name and Handicap Index so they can approve or decline.
  • TV board: an event's organizer can show its leaderboard on a TV or web page. Anyone with that event's code or link can see the players' names, scores and standings there, without signing in.
  • Course changes: corrections you make to a shared course are logged with your name so they can be reviewed.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions
    • Expo: app updates and notification delivery
    • Apple: Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, push notifications and Lock Screen live scores
    • RevenueCat: managing Clutch Up Pro subscriptions
    • Anthropic: reading scorecard photos and writing match and event stories from the scores. Under their commercial terms, data sent through their API isn't used to train their models by default.
    • GolfCourseAPI and OpenStreetMap: course search. Only the search text, or for "Near you" your approximate location, is sent, never your account details.
    • Sentry: crash reports, without your name, email or scores.
  • If required by law.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with our service providers, which may be outside Canada (for example, in the United States), and protected by their security measures, including encryption in transit.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Rounds and events you delete go to Recently Deleted, where they can be restored for 30 days before they're removed for good. When you delete your account, your profile, friends, the rounds and events you started, shared courses and requests are deleted, and if you used Sign in with Apple, we also disconnect Clutch Up from your Apple ID. Friends keep their own copies of rounds you played together. Reports you filed may be kept to protect the community. Our database provider's backups may hold copies for a short time after deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your details any time in the app (Profile, and More → Account Settings).
  • Delete your account in the app (More → Account Settings → Delete my account), or email us.
  • Turn notifications and Lock Screen live scores off in your phone's settings or in the app.
  • Turn off anonymous usage data in More → Account Settings → Preferences.
  • Manage or cancel Clutch Up Pro in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it by emailing us.

Children

Clutch Up isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
